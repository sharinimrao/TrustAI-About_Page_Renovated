Team

TrustAI is built by five MIT engineers and researchers with backgrounds at Google DeepMind, Jane Street, SpaceX, MIT Lincoln Laboratory, and CSAIL. The team includes International Physics and Astronomy Olympiad medalists, with published work at NeurIPS and ICML.

Advised by vLLM lead at Red Hat AI, Robert Shaw, and a Google Distinguished Engineer in AI Infrastructure.

02

Vision

Any model should be able to pick up where another left off. We're building toward AI systems that move context across models without recomputing it — making inference faster, cheaper, and more capable with every hop.

03

Mission

Make computed context transferable across the open-weight ecosystem. We build infrastructure that transfers state between models, so teams can use the best model for every task without paying the prefill cost twice — delivering inference at a fraction of the cost and time-to-first-token.
